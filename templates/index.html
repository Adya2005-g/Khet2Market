The challenge

Farmers deserve clearer paths to the market.

Price uncertainty and fragmented market access can leave growers without the information or negotiating power to make the most of their harvest.

  • Lack of transparent price information
  • Limited access to buyers
  • Dependence on intermediaries
  • Weak bargaining power
  • Post-harvest losses
  • Difficulty finding reliable buyers
The Khet2Market solution

A stronger link between farm gates and fair markets.

One accessible platform helps stakeholders discover prices, connect with verified counterparts and manage a transaction with greater visibility.
